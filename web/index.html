Clarity, built in

See exactly where your space went.

Every item is organized by app and purpose, measured precisely, and described in context. The largest files appear first — no vague health scores and no catch-all recommendations.

Applications

See an app and its related files together. Leftovers go to the Trash, keeping every removal reversible.

Browsers

Clear page and script caches from Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Brave, and Arc. History, passwords, bookmarks, and open tabs are never touched.

Creative

Review temporary media caches, previews, and scratch data from creative apps. Projects and exports are never listed for cleanup.

System

Review temporary macOS data, downloaded wallpapers, artwork caches, and logs that can quietly accumulate over time.

Developer tools

Find old simulators, build files, device support, and package caches. Every item is listed separately, so you can keep the versions you still need.

AI tools

Review caches and downloaded models from popular AI apps. Credentials are never listed, and sensitive session history is clearly flagged.

Control by design

Nothing leaves without your approval.

EasySweep makes every action explicit. Files that deserve extra care are clearly distinguished and excluded from automated cleanup.

Your approval comes first

Review the results, choose what you no longer need, and leave everything else exactly where it is.

Risk is visible

Archives, simulator devices, and session history are clearly flagged and excluded from automatic cleaning.

Matches are not certainties

Possible app leftovers are treated as matches, not facts. They move to the Trash so you can restore them if needed.

Honest about access

If EasySweep cannot read a folder, it says so and points to Full Disk Access instead of pretending there is nothing there.

Private by design

No telemetry, crash reporting, or background data collection. EasySweep does not collect or send information about you or your Mac.

Open to inspection

Every catalogued location is published on GitHub under MIT. Check what EasySweep knows, or add what it missed.
