Clarity before cleanup

Find the space your Mac leaves unexplained.

EasySweep brings scattered caches, downloads, old installers, and app leftovers into one clear view. Every item is measured and explained before you act — no vague health scores or catch-all recommendations.

Applications

Remove an app and review the files it left behind. Certain, likely, and possible matches stay separate, so a guess never looks like a fact.

Developer tools

Find old simulators, build files, device support, and package caches. Every item is listed separately, so you can keep the versions you still need.

AI tools

Review caches and downloaded models from popular AI apps. Credentials are never listed, and sensitive session history is clearly flagged.

Browsers

Clear page and script caches from Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Brave, and Arc. History, passwords, bookmarks, and open tabs are never touched.

Creative

Review temporary media caches, previews, and scratch data from creative apps. Projects and exports are never listed for cleanup.

System

Review temporary macOS data, downloaded wallpapers, artwork caches, and logs that can quietly accumulate over time.

Automatic, on your terms

Set the rules once. EasySweep watches the rest.

Decide what to watch, when cleanup should run, and how much to keep. EasySweep acts only when your conditions are met and records exactly what changed.

Act before space runs out

Choose your minimum free-space level. EasySweep monitors it from the menu bar and alerts you before a full disk interrupts your day.

A threshold for every item

Clean a cache when it grows past the limit that makes sense for it — not one blunt rule for your entire Mac.

Keep what is still current

Remove everything or only files older than a day, week, or month. Recent work can stay exactly where it is.

Waits for active apps

If a related app is running, unattended cleanup waits instead of removing files from underneath it.

Risk always stays manual

Archives, session history, and simulator devices are excluded from automation, regardless of the rule.

See the value over time

Track space reclaimed by date, item, and cleanup type, with a history of manual and automatic runs.

Safety you can verify

Careful by design, not by promise.

EasySweep checks again at the moment of deletion, separates certainty from guesswork, and reports what it cannot access. These safeguards live inside the cleaning engine, so a button or automation rule cannot quietly bypass them.

Rechecked before deletion

Every location is verified again when cleanup begins. If a path has changed or points somewhere unexpected, EasySweep refuses it.

Uninstalls show their evidence

Related files are grouped by match confidence. Uncertain matches remain visibly separate and never become automatic choices.

Reversible where it matters

Apps, discovered installers, and possible leftovers move to the Trash, so you have a way back if a match was not what you expected.

Honest about access

If EasySweep cannot read a folder, it says so and points to Full Disk Access instead of pretending there is nothing there.

Private by design

No telemetry, crash reporting, or background data collection. EasySweep does not collect or send information about you or your Mac.

Open-source scanning knowledge

Anyone can inspect where EasySweep looks. Catalogue updates are reviewed and pinned to a specific version before they reach the app.
